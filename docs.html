Why every model in the world is slow Written by the night shift, rack 07

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Warm GPU

A grey cat found asleep on a GPU tray in a data center. The tray is warm, the cat is heavy, the fans are blocked and the temperature has been rising since the night shift started. Every rack they check has one.

The site is the camera feed from rack 07 with the monitoring panel on top. Photoreal, cold blue aisle, warm orange glow, one cat.

Character
A chunky grey British Shorthair, asleep, ear moves when the flashlight hits it
Place
A hyperscale data center at night, rack 07, tray 03
The format
A live camera with three views and a temperature readout that never settles
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The temperature is a number in the page, the camera is a loop, the cat is asleep.

Music
